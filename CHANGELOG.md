# @styleeyes/g-element

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- [#4](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/pull/4) [`ba05d83`](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/commit/ba05d83e5491014a612051058ca280fc3182cad8) Thanks [@aaron-khroma](https://github.com/aaron-khroma)! - Add edges, wire names, breakpoint scopes, triggers, and the connection registry to core. Measurements now carry an `edge` (`off`, `rising`, `on`, `falling`) that settles once a frame has passed and every `holdEdge` hold is let go. `CircuitEnv` gains `requestFrame` and `cancelFrame`. New: `resolveWirePath`, which turns a wire name into the wire path the circuit uses (bundled into its device), `parseScope` for Tailwind-style breakpoint scopes, `parseTrigger` for trigger strings, and `connect` / `connections` / `probeConnections` on every circuit (also `createRegistry`).

- [#5](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/pull/5) [`1d27f27`](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/commit/1d27f27340ec3756208d6a77f0d12fa1000d1860) Thanks [@aaron-khroma](https://github.com/aaron-khroma)! - Add the React layer's foundation. `<GCircuit>` provides a circuit built from its `wires`, `buses`, and `breakpoints`; `<GDevice>` bundles the wires named inside it under its own name and installs its own schematic. `usePower`, `useSignal`, `useEdge`, `useWire`, and `useCircuit` read and change wires. `useReceiver` and `<GReceiver>` connect elements to wires with `data-g-power`, `data-g-edge`, `data-g-signal`, and optional `inert`, and hold each edge while the element's CSS transitions run. `Slot` gives a component's props to its child (`asChild`). Everything prerenders with the right starting states and hydrates without mismatches.

- [#3](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/pull/3) [`0c939d0`](https://github.com/aaron-khroma/g-element/commit/0c939d0e56214cc6a03683c3b3df8195c9265bb8) Thanks [@aaron-khroma](https://github.com/aaron-khroma)! - Rework the circuit store around latches and momentary drivers, with circuit vocabulary throughout. Wires resolve power and signal from a latch (`power`, `toggle`, `setSignal`) and from drivers (`drive`, `release` with an optional delay, sustain-only holds). `measure(wire)` returns a frozen `Measurement`, and `probe` / `probeAll` watch for changes. `trip` cuts a wire like a breaker. Schematics (`install`) declare starting states and buses with pullups, reference-counted. `everPowered` and injectable timers are new. `read`, `subscribe`, and `drive(wire, partial)` are replaced.
